Grainbridge, from village to modern city

Grainbridge through the ages

Each era adds one invention to the town: money, a bank, a government, a central bank, a stock market, a neighbour, research, and finally a whole modern economy. Every scene is a real run of the simulation, played back year by year: the town with the invention on the left, without it on the right.
