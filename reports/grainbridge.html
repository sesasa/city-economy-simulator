    How Grainbridge works

    Farming

    Each farmer grows 5 sacks of wheat a year. Feeding 150 people takes 30 farmers.

    The factory

    Turns wheat into bread, up to 1,000 loaves a year. It needs a crew of 50 just to open its doors, plus 1 worker for every 10 loaves.

    Deliveries

    One carter for every 10 loaves brought to people's homes.

    Repairs

    A factory needs 30 people to stay in shape. Without them it loses up to 5% of its strength a year. Irrigation and tools need upkeep too.

    Building

    Spare hands can dig irrigation (more wheat per farmer), make tools (fewer workers per loaf) or build a new factory when the old one is nearly full.

    Rest

    Whatever is left is free time, the town's real wealth. More free time means people are better off.
